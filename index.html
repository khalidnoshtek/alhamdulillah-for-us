For Hafsah

Some moments
are quieter in close.

Best with headphones

tap to continue

Some love stories begin with ease.

Ours survived storms.

And somehow…

we still chose each other.

Happy Birthday

Hafsah

Our story was never easy.

People doubted us.

People tried to separate us.

There was a time you were already my wife…

but I could not even speak to you.

A whole year passed.

No calls.

No messages.

No way to know if you were okay.

But even distance
could not break
what Allah had written.

Hafsah,

you suffered in silence.

you were hurt for choosing me.

you carried pain that nobody
should have carried alone.

And still…

you never let go of us.

When my health became weak…

when life became heavy…

you cared for me with a love
I cannot explain.

You stayed beside me through
moments I had nothing to offer.

There was a moment you told me…

"You can leave me
and start a new life."

But how could I leave the one who
stayed loyal through everything?

And somehow…

between pain, distance,
responsibilities, struggle…

we built a home.

Not perfect. But filled with
loyalty, sabr, and love.

I watched you become
a mother.

Even exhausted, you gave our children
warmth, patience, and love.

The kids are lucky to have you.

And I am lucky Allah wrote you for me.

Hafsah,

you gave me loyalty.

you gave me patience.

you gave me peace
during my worst moments.

And wallahi…

I will spend my life
trying to honor that.

I cannot rewrite
the difficult chapters.

But I promise to make
the next chapters softer.

As long as Allah keeps us together…

you will never walk alone,
Hafsah.

Alhamdulillah for us.

Thank you for staying,
Hafsah.

with all of me

A note for you

One last thing…

Hafsah, there are some things
I could never write down.
So I said them out loud — for only you.
