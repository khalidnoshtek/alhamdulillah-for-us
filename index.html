My love

Scroll

Our story was never easy.

People doubted us.

People tried to separate us.

There was a time when you were already my wife…

but I could not even speak to you.

A whole year passed.

No calls.

No messages.

No way to know if you were okay.

But even distance could not break what Allah had written.

What you carried

You stayed.

You suffered in silence.

You were hurt for choosing me.

You carried pain that nobody should have carried alone.

And still…

you never let go of us.

I know our wedding was not the way you deserved.

I know there were moments where you felt alone.

When my health became weak…

when life became heavy…

you cared for me with a love I cannot explain.

You stayed beside me during moments where I had nothing to offer.

There was a moment where you told me…

"You can leave me and start a new life."

But how could I leave the person who stayed loyal through everything?

And somehow…

between pain, distance, responsibilities, loneliness, and struggles…

we built a home.

Not a perfect one.

But one filled with loyalty, sabr, and love.

Her, as a mother

I watched you become a mother.

And I watched you carry responsibilities that should never have been yours alone.

Even while exhausted…

you still gave our children warmth, patience, and love.

I know you deserved more support than you received.

The kids are lucky to have you.

And I am lucky Allah wrote you for me.

You gave me loyalty.

You gave me patience.

You gave me peace during my worst moments.

And wallahi…

I will spend my life trying to honor that.

I cannot rewrite the difficult chapters.

But I promise to make the next chapters softer.

As long as Allah keeps us together…

you will never walk alone.

Alhamdulillah for us.

Thank you for staying.
